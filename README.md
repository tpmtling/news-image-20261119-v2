A classic newspaper front page that sums up Thursday 19 November 2026 in one 1024x1024 image: the first-ever Trump-Xi-Putin trilateral summit in Shenzhen leads, with tankers burning after mine strikes east of Hormuz, Israel's rare ground operation in central Gaza, a record $190.4 million Van Gogh, and the worldwide GTA VI launch in the columns, plus a four-item briefs strip. The picture is drawn deterministically with Pillow by `render.py` from the hand-picked stories in `news_data.md`; `main.py` (standard library only) copies the finished `news_image.png` to `/output/entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
